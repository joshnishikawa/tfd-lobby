Tabletop Sandbox — Controls

Tabletop Navigation

  • Space + Drag or Middle-Click Drag: Pan camera
  • Mouse Wheel or Navbar Zoom Slider: Zoom in/out
  • Center View: Fit camera to all tabletop objects

Object Controls

  • Drag: Move cards, tokens, decks, and zones
  • Right-Click: Context menu (Draw, Shuffle, Peek, Lock, Take to Hand)
  • F: Flip selected card(s) · T: Tap 90° · R: Rotate +90°
  • Hold Alt: High-resolution card preview
