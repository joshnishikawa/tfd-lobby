Tic Tac Toe — Rules

Normal Mode

Take turns placing X and O on a 3×3 grid. The first player to align 3 marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.

Ultimate Mode

  • The board consists of 9 nested 3×3 sub-boards arranged in a larger 3×3 grid.
  • Whichever cell position you play inside a sub-board sends your opponent to the corresponding sub-board on the main grid.
  • If the target sub-board is already won or full, the opponent may play in any open sub-board.
  • Win 3 sub-boards in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on the main grid to win the match.
